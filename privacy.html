Privacy Policy

Maps Lead Extractor (Chrome extension). Last updated: October 5, 2026.

Summary

The extension has no server and no account system. Everything it collects stays in your browser. We do not sell, share or receive your data.

What the extension reads

  • Google Maps search results you are viewing: business name, category, rating, review count, phone, website, address and coordinates. These are read only on Google Maps pages and only so you can export them.
  • Business websites (optional, Pro): when you press "Find emails", the extension visits the public website of each business in your list to find publicly listed contact emails and social profile links. Chrome asks for your permission before this happens.

Where the data is stored

Results are saved in your browser's local extension storage (chrome.storage.local) until you press "Clear all leads" or uninstall the extension. Exports are files created on your computer. Nothing is uploaded to us.

License validation

If you buy Pro, the license key you paste is sent to Lemon Squeezy (our payment provider) to verify it. Lemon Squeezy processes your purchase under its own privacy policy. We receive the order details Lemon Squeezy provides to sellers (name, email, country) only to deliver and support your purchase.

What we don't do

  • No analytics, tracking or advertising.
  • No browsing history collection. The extension only runs on Google Maps pages and, if you ask for it, on the business websites in your list.
  • No selling or transferring of user data to third parties, and no use of data for creditworthiness or lending.

Your responsibility

You are responsible for using exported business information in line with the laws that apply to you (for example anti-spam and data protection laws) and with Google's terms.
